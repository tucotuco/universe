Universe · Live combat

A real Encounter advances only when you issue a tactical command. All HP, equipment, reactions, actions and XP belong to persistent Universe Entities. Each command advances one second; strikes test reach only at impact.

Personal-scale encounter · top-down

Allocate weapon skill effort

Spend up to 2 Dagger skill points among attack accuracy, defense, timing and extra damage. These affect your next attack; Guard uses the chosen defense allocation (or the default of 2 when unset). Moving does not interrupt an action.

Offense · timed attacks

Defense · stance or timed reaction

Parry and Dodge are one-second reactions. They protect against an attack that completes in that same second, and interrupt an unfinished attack. Dodge also moves you away. Assign Defense effort above if desired. Actual threatened attempts earn XP.

Current live state

You can move while an attack is pending. Swing, thrust, Guard, Parry, Dodge or Cancel interrupt an unfinished attack; interrupted attempts earn no XP. Guard persists during movement; reactions take one second and earn practice only against a real imminent attack. Diagonal steps are normalized to the same distance. The Raider follows a provisional deterministic tactical policy; it isn't a fully autonomous Agent.

Opponent · Perceived state

Your own strikes report exact HP damage dealt and a qualitative share of estimated opponent endurance, but not the opponent's current or maximum HP. A distance and reach indication is shown only with a current unobstructed geometric path observation. Reach and obstruction are tested when a strike resolves, not when it is scheduled. This model is not yet physical vision.

Actions and consequences

    Current model boundaries

    Reach, speed and body geometry remain explicit provisional units. There is no collision avoidance, facing, anatomical hit locations, general multi-attacker parrying, full simultaneous damage model, or validated optical gesture recognition. Versioned command-journal saves reconstruct the encounter under the same simulation code; cross-version migrations and arbitrary world-state serialization remain deferred. Same-time melee attempts use frozen prerequisites; effects are applied in deterministic order. All observations, including geometric paths, remain distinct from belief.
