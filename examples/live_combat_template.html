    Simulation notes

    Arrow keys move the Scout north, south, east, and west. Diagonal movement remains available from the action panel. Movement does not interrupt a pending attack, Guard, or a prepared reaction. Click a Being to inspect its available details, or hover for a brief summary. Parry and Dodge target an apparent incoming attack and must be ready by impact. Opponent state remains observer-filtered.

    Reach and obstruction are checked at impact. Facing, precise anatomy, general multi-attacker parrying, full simultaneous consequences, and arbitrary world-state serialization remain deferred. The Raider follows a provisional policy.

    Allocate Dagger skill effort

    Allocate up to 2 skill points between accuracy, defense, timing, and damage. The allocation applies to the next attack; Guard uses the defense allocation (or its default).
