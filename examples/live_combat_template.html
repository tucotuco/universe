A real Encounter advances only when you issue a tactical command. All HP, equipment, reactions, actions and XP belong to persistent Universe Entities. Each command advances one second; strikes test reach only at impact.

Allocate weapon skill effort

Spend up to 2 Dagger skill points among attack accuracy, defense, timing and extra damage. These affect your next attack; Guard uses the chosen defense allocation (or the default of 2 when unset). Moving does not interrupt an action.

Defense · stance or timed reaction

Target the currently perceived incoming strike. Parry requires 3 seconds of dagger preparation; Dodge requires 2 seconds. Both must be ready at that strike’s impact. A late request is rejected without interrupting your existing action. Dodge displaces at impact. Actual threatened attempts earn XP.

Current live state

You can move while an attack is pending. Swing, thrust, Guard, Parry, Dodge or Cancel interrupt an unfinished attack; interrupted attempts earn no XP. Guard persists during movement; reactions have weapon-based or two-second preparation times and earn practice only against their designated strike. Diagonal steps are normalized to the same distance. The Raider follows a provisional deterministic tactical policy; it isn't a fully autonomous Agent.

Actions and consequences

    Current model boundaries

    Reach, speed and body geometry remain explicit provisional units. There is no collision avoidance, facing, anatomical hit locations, general multi-attacker parrying, full simultaneous damage model, or validated optical gesture recognition. Versioned command-journal saves reconstruct the encounter under the same simulation code; cross-version migrations and arbitrary world-state serialization remain deferred. Same-time melee attempts use frozen prerequisites; effects are applied in deterministic order. All observations, including geometric paths, remain distinct from belief.
