    Rules coverage · what this duel does and does not simulate

    Implemented in this encounter: one-second steps, timed dagger/longsword swing and thrust, bounded strategic effort, guarded stance, target-specific Parry/Dodge, eight-direction movement, fatigue checks, HP, armor, and weapon XP. The human and autonomous opponent use the same rule resolver.

    Not yet fully implemented: surprise/hearing, hex-based facing/flanking, calibrated weapon reach, ranged/unarmed combat, grappling, special attacks, all fatigue and equipment rules, six-second rounds, and exact simultaneous physical consequences. The authority is docs/Encounters Working Draft.pdf. Consult docs/ai-encounter-experiment.md for the section-by-section matrix.

    Simulation notes

    Arrow keys move the Scout north, south, east, and west. Diagonal movement remains available from the action panel. Movement does not interrupt a pending attack, Guard, or a prepared reaction. Click a Being to inspect its available details, or hover for a brief summary. Parry and Dodge target an apparent incoming attack and must be ready by impact. Opponent state remains observer-filtered.

    Reach and obstruction are checked at impact. Facing, precise anatomy, engagement rolls, general multi-attacker parrying, full simultaneous consequences, and arbitrary world-state serialization remain deferred. The opponent and player receive the same action-readiness checks and select from the same bounded melee commands and uses the same authoritative resolution engine. The deterministic policy is a baseline; the optional LLM proposes actions but cannot alter combat mechanics. This does not yet implement all rules in the Encounters Working Draft.

    Allocate effort

    Allocate up to 2 skill points as you select the action. Once it begins, this allocation is fixed until you explicitly choose a new action.
