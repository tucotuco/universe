## 2. What Universe is building; authority

Universe builds a coherent world by **resolution on demand**: coarse statistical commitments constrain later localized individual, spatial, and historical realization. Refine only what an inquiry or Action requires, retaining existing truth, identities, correlations, and provenance. This is a direction with bounded implementations, not a finished cross-domain constraint solver.

Objective **Reality**, observer-relative **Observation**, and evolving **Belief** are separate. Different perceptions or testimony can disagree without rewriting objective facts. Shared inquiries use compatible shared candidate truth; incompatible objective proposals reject atomically. World generation/materialization does not automatically grant Awareness, observations, parentage knowledge or Belief. Awareness alone is not a full evidence/Belief representation.

Beings are native independently existing Objects; Events remain a distinct ontology. `StatisticalGroup(ObjectInstance)` is a population prototype with unresolved slots and conserved counts, not a general Group ontology. `ReproductionGroup` is an implemented logical reproductive-parent membership/offspring construct, distinct from population, household, residence, social partnership or command organization. Native materialization and population/place association require explicit separate authority.

Continuous 3D Cartesian position is authoritative; ISEA TriFold context and Encounter grids are derived. Local spatial examples do not establish global geography/terrain consistency.

## 4. Settled invariants to preserve

- **One identity across layers:** no duplicate Person when binding a logical offspring, counted slot and native Being. Population totals remain conserved. Parentage does not establish residence, population membership, place, or observer knowledge.
- **ReproductionGroup existence:** at least one offspring; one or more known parent members, possibly incomplete. `parents_complete` refers to parent membership, not offspring enumeration. Complete singleton `{A}` and incomplete known singleton `{A}` differ. Zero outcomes record request/horizon facts without constructing an empty group.
- **Exact-set identity and merging:** only complete unordered exact parent sets uniquely index a live group. Overlapping incomplete groups remain distinct. Completing H to complete G's exact set merges into G atomically: union offspring, reindex derived lookup, retire H from live groups, reserve H as canonical alias, preserve historical H receipts and original provenance. Replays return the original receipt without extra revision/draw; conflicting reuse refuses. No second parentage ledger overrides membership.
- **Transactions and reproducibility:** detached preparation is read-only; validate combined candidates and revisions before publication. Stable deterministic sampling and request identity; exception/fault rollback preserves containers and held references. No automatic reroll to hide contradiction, uncontrolled retry or unapproved biological rule.
- **Encounter:** integer-second intervals; movement first, common post-movement/pre-Action snapshot, evaluate simultaneous outcomes, atomically publish effects/RNG/lifecycle/practice. Legitimate misses/failures commit; exceptional integrity faults abort the whole current second and require correction before deterministic retry. Prior committed seconds persist.
- **Choices/resources/results:** whole choices accepted/replaced/undone atomically before execution; explicit active Object-use reservations, no blanket Being reservation merely for actor/target status. Multiple designated defenses preserve associations. Targets own resulting state/history; Actions retain causes/effects, not duplicate HP-before/after. UI notification follows commitment. Native implementation subset and adopted general requirements are distinct.
- **Discovery:** deliberate Search/Spot/Listen are discovery Actions, not progressive skills; ordinarily six seconds, target-owned DC default 15, no Spot XP. The older provisional `spot` skill and Group sampler do not supersede r60. No automatically shared Awareness or Belief from world publication.
- **Persistence:** version-compatible committed checkpoints, stable references and atomic validation. Existing bounded formats do not satisfy the general authoritative-state closure requirement. Fatigue integration remains separate: Q/B only, PCFS=5; do not activate R4 workbench settlement without authorization.
