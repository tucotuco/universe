# Adopted Encounter supplement — 2026-11-15

This dated supplement extends the effective Encounter rules after revision 70. It does not revise or rewrite the historical revision-61 baseline. Source adoption record: [multiple-shield passive-protection adoption](adoptions/2026-11-15-multiple-shield-passive-protection.md).

## Multiple-shield passive protection

When a Being legally bears more than one shield providing passive protection, each shield has its own attack-coverage capacity equal to that shield's size. A single incoming attack may receive passive protection from at most one shield; interception chances, stopped damage, and durability effects do not stack against it.

Before attack outcomes are determined, the defender assigns eligible incoming attacks to individual borne shields without exceeding each shield's capacity. The existing prior-round-observable restriction applies: only incoming Actions perceptible to the defender in the preceding round may be deliberately assigned. An Action first revealed during the resolving round cannot be selected retroactively. Allocation is not a timed Action and consumes no Action-capacity resource.

Each assigned attack uses only its assigned shield's normal passive-interception procedure. Durability loss applies to that shield's persistent Object identity. Same-boundary attacks use the shared post-movement/pre-Action snapshot of shield eligibility, size, durability, and allocation; resulting effects commit together. Combined damage that destroys a shield does not retroactively cancel protection valid at that boundary; later boundaries use committed shield state.

All existing equipment, hand, buckler, tower-shield, Active Block, awareness, and physical-legality requirements remain in force. This supplement does not make it automatically legal to wield two shields, nor permit one shield to occupy two hands or places. It introduces no automatic priority between equally eligible shields, additional sensing, coverage for previously unseen attacks, additional defense roll, or new geometry. Where an eligible multi-shield declaration does not identify which shield applies, preserve fail-closed behavior until the missing choice is specified.

This is an adopted rule, not implementation evidence. See `TASKS.md` and the Action coverage matrix for current implementation status.
