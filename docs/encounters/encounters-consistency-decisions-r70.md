# Revision 70 — zero-distance Disarm landing

**Adopted:** 14 November 2026, by explicit user decision. **Applies to:** revision 61 as supplemented by revisions 62–69.

When a successful Disarm margin of 1 or 2 permits zero hexes of travel, the disarmed Weapon may land in the defender's occupied hex. This is the sole occupied-cell exception for Disarm landing. The Weapon remains the same native Object, with no displacement from the defender's cell. Other occupied cells and unknown locations remain illegal; if another Being or ground Object also occupies the defender's cell, the landing is not available. For positive-range landings, choose only a represented unoccupied cell within the roll-derived maximum distance.

This ruling resolves only the zero-distance landing case. It does not change Disarm roll, timing, custody, unarmed take, or subsequent interaction rules.
