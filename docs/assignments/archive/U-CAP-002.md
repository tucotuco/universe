## Workstream B — Playable passage, withdrawal and prone maneuvers

- **End Run:** Adopted **three-second preparation**, with passage attempted **in the movement phase** of its completion second, before non-movement Action resolution. Passing through a person's occupied hex is unavailable beforehand. Test willing occupant plus clear onward hex; resisting occupant with the **3d6+Dexterity-bonus** contest and **+2 per size category smaller** for the initiator; tie/failure leaves initiator in its starting hex; success exits into a different unoccupied legal hex (not back to origin). Include behind/immobilized **3d6 ≤ Dexterity** branch, modified action timing, unknown/full occupancy fail-closed, changing occupants, interruption, and simultaneous interactions.
- **Disengage:** Ordinary movement can withdraw only through a legal, unoccupied/open route; engagement depends on **directional facing and relative reach**. Do not invent an opportunity attack or automatically reduce the withdrawer's defense to zero. A scheduled opposing strike checks **post-movement reach** and normal defenses. Demonstrate at least one withdrawal that defeats a strike by leaving range and one that remains in range. Do not elevate Disengage to an invented movement-free instantaneous attack/defense Action if current rules represent it as movement.
- **Overrun:** Adopted **four-second** attempt and relevant action/strategy modifiers, with Bull Rush's applicable opposed Strength mechanics **plus Dexterity modifiers on both sides**, shield/size/stability terms and on-success target **prone in the occupied hex**. Implement straightforward supported two-Being standing cases. Honor a legal +1 following charge/bull rush with **additional two seconds** where the actual prerequisite is represented. Do not invent Trample, mount geometry or an unsupported displacement/collision resolution. Treat unspecified geometry distinctly from a failed contest.
- **Bull Rush:** Implement the **one-second** declaration, attack/defense level 0 during the maneuver, willing-target and resisted **3d6+Strength bonus** contests, +4 size differential, +2 stability/multi-leg when facts exist, target-too-large restriction, shield proficiency bonus, Yield nullification, forward direction and subsequent-second +1 continuation where possible. Preserve independent concerted rush and mounted cases as bounded if their full participant/geometry contracts are absent. A successful contest does **not** license invented Newtons or displacement. If the adopted text and actual legal positions/speeds determine an exact supported target movement, publish it transactionally; otherwise expose a typed successful-contest/pending-physical-displacement boundary or fail closed for that branch, with an explicit evidence-based rule question. Do not introduce an arbitrary one-hex shove or repurpose Pull's unresolved force-to-motion mapping.
