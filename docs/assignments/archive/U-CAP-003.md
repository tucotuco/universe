## A. Draw, Ready, hands and lawful weapon use

- Keep **Draw** a separate Action: normally 2 seconds to make a weapon in a represented drawable location available, and **does not Ready** it. Preserve the adopted Quick-draw-plus-advanced-skill exceptional timing without silently inventing a zero-duration Action or making it an ordinary 2-second action. A carried, picked-up or unlocated Object is **not automatically drawable**. A picked-up weapon may become usable only by an explicit, lawful native custody/equipment pathway with supported hand and timing facts.
- **Ready** is preparation for possible use, never a condition, target commitment or trigger watcher. It is specific to eligible weapon motion with time `floor(T/3)`, reduces that subsequent motion's time by the same amount with 1-second minimum, survives Delay, and is consumed on the next other Action using that weapon. A weapon may be wielded without advance Ready at normal timing. Preserve revision-69 pause/resume and native identity semantics. Avoid implicit cross-motion preparation or a hidden automatic re-ready step.
- Weapon size relative to Being size determines light/one-hand/two-hand/impossible use. For a size category one larger than the Being, **two hands are required but no two-handed damage bonus**; more than one larger is unusable. Same-size weapons may be used one-handed or in two hands according to adopted Two-handed strike. Use *explicit* hand availability and native Object ownership. Test non-Dagger weapons, weapon changes, multi-Action simultaneous hand conflicts, ineligible occupancy, repeated attacks and readiness consumption. Do not silently create hands for native Beings that lack them.
- **Two-handed strike:** only where hands and shield occupancy actually permit it; where the weapon is not designed for two-handed use, apply the specified `floor(1.5 × weapon damage)` after ordinary damage roll, following exact rule semantics; no bonus for a weapon designed for two hands. Bearing a non-buckler shield prohibits this strike. Treat buckler as a documented exception with its own same-hand modifier. Preserve attack-motion reach, timing, current damage/armor/critical pipeline and simultaneous effects.

## B. Shields, weapon-like equipment and resource conflicts

- Complete the **lawful Shield bash** for supported equipped shields: use native shield attack-motion timing, damage and penetration type; ordinary bash bludgeons, spiked alternatives pierce where explicitly represented, and applicable off-hand/Ambidexterity modifiers from the adopted rules. Do not invent an undefined off-hand number or a spontaneous spiked shield. A tower shield *borne for protection* cannot bash and also prohibits Charge, Bull Rush and Overrun as already adopted.
- Apply the adopted differences between a **buckler** (forearm attachment with restricted but possible hand use and same-hand attack penalty) and **small/medium/large shields** (arm and hand occupied solely by shield), with no fake hand availability. Preserve passive shield protection, interception allocation against simultaneous attacks, durability, blocking, and eligibility at the common pre-Action snapshot. Prevent a single hand/arm/weapon from being double-booked without lawful Multiple actions and resource allocation.
- Support wearing/removing a shield, and other equipment transitions **where authoritative native source/hand/strap and applicable time are represented**. Consult the adopted armor/shield timing table (`Don Shield ST×5`, `Remove Shield ST×2`; armor as listed) and current implementation before adding generic don/doff. Do not infer a standard ST, a free hand, armor fit, or an instantaneous transfer. If unsupported categories remain, expose bounded refusal and track them in `TASKS.md` rather than pretending to equip them.
