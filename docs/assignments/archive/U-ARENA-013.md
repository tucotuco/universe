# U-ARENA-013 — Attack-only opponent approaches and faces its target

**Repository:** Universe (Codex-owned)<br>
**Type:** Outcome-oriented, playable native-Arena increment<br>
**Install at:** `docs/assignments/U-ARENA-013.md`<br>
**Report:** `docs/reports/U-ARENA-013-report.md`

## Outcome

Make the existing **Attack only (native Dagger)** opponent capable of pursuing an *observed* player and attaining an ordinary, legal attack opportunity in the **unobstructed, empty-ground** generated Arena. From an initially separated or improperly facing position, the opponent should select and execute real native movement/turn-and-move as necessary, then submit its own native Thrust when appropriate. The player can move and attack independently; progression remains one-second, both sides' actions follow the native scheduler, and Save/Load yields deterministic continuation.

This is a useful opponent, **not** a full tactical AI. Do not implement the other strategy options in this assignment. Do not make a combat hit certain, invent a body geometry, or circumvent the native movement and Action systems.

## Current verified baseline

- U-ARENA-009 integrated `npcgen.api.arena_ready_being(...)`: the opponent has its own real native readied Dagger and immutable initial Generator provenance.
- U-ARENA-011 enabled native hex-occupancy reach against Beings at **impact**: penetration into the target's occupied regular one-yard hex permits ordinary strike resolution, but does not guarantee a hit. Its `not_facing`, `out_of_reach`, `blocked`, and `uncertain_path` outcomes remain meaningful. Being 3-D geometry is deferred for LOS, cover, thrown Objects, and missile weapons.
- U-ARENA-012 introduced the attack-only opponent: a fixed native Thrust, gated on its own awareness/evidence, capacity, and actual ready Dagger; no movement or turning; opponent action visibility is observer-authorized and delayed. `/5` Arena saves include the strategy, and `/4` saves load with autonomy disabled. Pending native opponent actions are persisted and not regenerated.
- The ordinary starter ground profile can produce `uncertain_path` from unmodeled ground Objects. **Keep that conservative behavior.** Use `Empty ground` for acceptance, and do not interpret unmodeled Objects as transparent.
- Preserve authoritative continuous metric Cartesian positions, one-yard regular-hex occupancy/display, twelve facings, selected speed modes, distinct turn-and-move vs maintain-facing movement, movement-before-Action boundary evaluation, observer-owned evidence, and transaction/rollback semantics.

## Implementation

1. **Inspect existing native contracts first.** Read the three reports and applicable rule package. Trace `GeneratedArenaSession`, the U-ARENA-012 decision seam, NPC observer evidence, the native destination-plan/speed/facing movement APIs, simultaneous movement-plus-Action behavior, one-second stepping, and the hex reach/facing evaluator. Reuse native operations. Do not add an AI-owned world model, an independent clock, or a second movement simulator.
2. **Minimal deterministic attack-only decision.** On a lawful native decision opportunity, use **only the opponent's current observer-authorized information** to identify/locate the player; no access to uncommitted player movement destinations or proposed Actions, and no automatic knowledge of lost targets. If a supported native Thrust is tactically plausible from the current committed snapshot (weapon ready, forward facing, and hex-reach opportunity), submit through the existing native action path. Otherwise, when legal, choose a native turn-and-move/destination plan toward the observed target to improve range or facing. Eligibility at **impact** remains authoritative; an opponent can still miss or encounter a moved target. Avoid repeatedly scheduling doomed distant/backward attacks merely because scheduling permits them. A native failure to move or lack of a lawful movement/turn contract must be handled explicitly, not bypassed.
3. **Movement constraints.** Implement the simplest reproducible, unobstructed-map approach using the existing continuous coordinates and native movement facilities; no pathfinding, no collision assumptions, teleporting, instant facing edits, or extra movement Actions. Use valid in-map destinations with deterministic ties. Respect native speed, turn restrictions, plan cancellation/replacement, and the ability (where adopted) to move during a demanding Action. Do not synthesize free turning in place if the engine lacks a legal primitive. Do not trample an accepted active movement/Action or reissue identical orders every second. Treat the player's future movement as unknown.
4. **Decision/persistence safety.** Autonomous decisions must occur at native permitted decision times, in correct relation to player submissions and the common snapshot, without knowledge of future actions. Preserve atomic proposal/validation/publication and refusal-without-mutation guarantees. Reuse the existing selected strategy; persist any **genuinely necessary** new per-opponent controller state with a strict, versioned representation. Prefer deriving policy decisions from committed native state and authorized evidence over creating extra duplicated state. On Load, do not re-run a decision already accepted or generate new identities/equipment; subsequent choices must replay deterministically.
5. **UI and observability.** Retain the Generate strategy selection and ordinary player controls. Show only movement/facing/Actions the player can observe, using existing opponent-specific projection; avoid leaking the opponent's intended private destination, hidden inventory, native IDs, or source NPC facts. The terminal trace may describe the high-level AI choice for debugging without private identifiers. The player should be able to see the opponent approach and initiate a real attack without manually moving/turning the opponent.
6. **Protect scope and rules.** The attack-only label describes proactively selected Actions; do not suppress normal native defensive resolution. Do not invent damage bonuses, target future-position predictions, body shapes, obstruction/cover rules, new weapon policies, sophisticated tactics, victory conditions, or the random/defense-only/mixed strategies. Do not change `npcgen`.

## Acceptance proof

- **End-to-end positive demonstration:** From a reproducibly seeded, equipped, two-Being generated Encounter on an initially unobstructed 10×5 map with `Empty ground`, show an opponent initially separated and/or misfacing the player. Without editing native positions/facings/HP/equipment/awareness after generation, and without manually moving the opponent, advance through ordinary browser/native controls. Demonstrate the opponent's **native** approach/turn-and-move, a subsequent accepted `MeleeStrike` using its exact API-created Dagger, and a genuine native impact result. A hit is **not** required; record the actual result and relevant distance/facing and timing. A stationary player is sufficient for the core proof, with a separate moving-player check.
- **Boundary controls:** Verify in-range facing allows an attack without needless new movement, misfacing or distant cases choose legal approach rather than repeated predictably invalid Thrusts, and an absent/unready weapon, lost awareness/evidence, unable actor, or pending Action/plan is handled without invented proposals or hidden target knowledge. Confirm the opponent cannot read the player's uncommitted destination/Action.
- **Native chronology:** Verify player movement during opponent approach and during an already accepted Thrust, including impact-time reach/facing and independent player attack resolution. Rejected movement/action proposals leave time, events, ChoiceBook, and RNG unchanged.
- **Cross-process persistence:** Save while the opponent is approaching and again with an opponent Thrust pending. Restore each into a fresh session with NPC generation APIs stubbed to fail; verify exact native plan/Action/Object identities, both sides' positions/facings, observer histories, event/Choice state, HP, RNG cursor, and eventual outcomes against uninterrupted continuation. Verify completed saves and prior `/4`/`/5` strategy-load behavior remain intact.
- **Browser smoke:** In Chrome (if loopback permitted), demonstrate the visible autonomous approach, accepted attack, time advancement, and observer-safe result. Distinguish tested HTTP/JavaScript paths from actual real-browser actions. Use the empty-ground profile; keep starter-profile `uncertain_path` as a known limitation.
- **Focused validation:** Run new/affected tests and a small adjacent regression selection, syntax checks, and `git diff --check`; do not run the hour-long suite for this bounded milestone. Give exact commands, test counts, timings, and failures/limits without contradictory totals.
