# U-ARENA-014 — First playable native defense in the generated Arena

**Repository:** `universe` (Codex-owned)<br>
**Type:** Outcome-oriented, end-to-end Arena feature<br>
**Install as:** `docs/assignments/U-ARENA-014.md`<br>
**Report:** `docs/reports/U-ARENA-014-report.md`

## Outcome

In an ordinary generated, two-Being Arena with the **Empty ground** profile, a player can use an actually supported **native defensive choice** against an observed opponent strike, and a separately selectable **Defense only** opponent can defend against an observed player strike without attacking. At least one genuine defensive interaction must traverse native declaration, preparation/availability, strike resolution, observer projection, and deterministic Save/Load. Use real generated Beings, their own NPC Generator-created Dagger equipment where applicable, and ordinary browser controls. Do not create a second damage/defense engine or fabricate a favorable defense outcome.

Keep this a playable vertical slice; **mixed** and **random** opponent strategies remain for a later milestone. If inspection reveals that no native defense has a sufficiently implemented, rule-compatible contract, provide a focused executable native proof and precise blocker rather than inventing defense rules. Prefer implementing a narrow legitimate defensive path over a framework.

## Grounded starting point

- Read `docs/handoffs/universe-8-to-9.md`, the U-ARENA-009 through U-ARENA-013 engineering reports, and the authoritative Encounter rules and precedence documentation. In particular, verify revision-61 defense preparation, readiness, expenditure, target selection, simultaneous commitment, and constraints against the **current** code; historical prototype patches and examples are not authority.
- U-ARENA-011 supports hex-occupancy melee targetability for Beings at impact. It does not guarantee hits or replace body geometry for line-of-sight, cover, thrown Objects, missiles, or collision.
- U-ARENA-012/013 give the attack-only opponent native Thrust and movement/turn-and-move. Its decisions use only its own observer evidence and committed state. Do not regress this behavior or let a defensive opponent read the player's uncommitted Action, destination, or RNG future.
- Ground Objects of unknown geometry may still produce `uncertain_path`; keep them conservatively modeled. Use `Empty ground` in positive acceptance tests.
- Preserve the source NPC profiles, API equipment provenance, native Being/Object ownership, exact continuous positions and twelve-way facing, movement-before-Action chronology, all-Action shared pre-action snapshot, deterministic RNG/Choice publication and rollback, private observation, and strict Save/Load without regeneration.

## Implementation

1. **Inspect and select the smallest lawful defensive mechanism.** Identify current production contracts for Guard, Parry, Dodge, defensive strategy allocation, or other applicable native defenses: preparation duration, readiness/consumption, instrument and skill requirements, awareness of a threat, selection among incoming strikes, eligibility at impact, and result reporting. Decide which one yields the smallest legitimate browser-to-native feature; report why. Do not equate a defensive *opponent strategy label* with the native `Strategy` effort/allocation object. Do not treat an obsolete one-second prototype defense as adopted revision-61 timing. No invented default defense bonus, automatic success, or instantaneous free Parry/Dodge.
2. **Player control.** Expose the chosen supported defensive choice in the player's existing contextual Action surface, with clear eligibility, preparation/readiness or sustained status, and ordinary refusal feedback. Resolve target/incoming-strike references server-side from current observer-authorized handles/evidence; never reveal hidden native Action IDs or opponent source facts. The player's existing attacks, destination plans, pickup/draw, and one-second Advance must remain usable. A defense that is already prepared must not be spent merely because time advances; expenditure and replacement follow adopted rules.
3. **Defense-only opponent.** Enable exactly the agreed `Defense only` strategy in Generate Encounter (retain `No autonomous strategy` and `Attack only`; leave `Mixed`/`Random` unavailable). Give the opponent a deterministic, minimal policy that prepares/maintains the selected native defense when lawful and commits it against an *observed* eligible attack only when timing and native contract permit. It must not submit its own strikes or secretly inspect the player's unpublished intentions. If unobserved, unable, unready, or missing necessary equipment, it waits or refuses cleanly; there must be no phantom Choice or RNG consumption. Never force a positive defense outcome for demonstration.
4. **Native chronology and conflicts.** Use native Action scheduling, validation, reservation/Choice, defensive preparation/expenditure, simultaneous proposal evaluation, movement-before-Action, and atomic publication. Exercise at least one strike/defense completion boundary, including a defense that is too late or unavailable. Defending must not retroactively cancel a simultaneously resolving lawful attack. No direct HP, result, effort, facing, Action, or observer-evidence edits from the session/UI.
5. **Observation and display.** Show the player only its own defensive selection/status and permitted evidence of the opponent's defense/strike; expose readiness and outcome when observable. No opponent HP, hidden intended defense, native IDs, private equipment/skills, or unpublished intentions. Retain useful high-level terminal traces without printing hidden identifiers.
6. **Determinism, persistence, migration.** Ensure saved selections, prepared/active defenses, their native identities, expiry/expenditure, evidence, RNG, and relevant Choice state restore exactly. Load must not invoke `npcgen` or `arena_ready_being`; old saves must continue to load with their previous strategy semantics. Avoid adding controller state if deterministic choices can be derived from native state. If a version change is necessary, validate it strictly and test the migration/default. Preserve existing attack-only approach and pending-Thrust Save/Load.

## Acceptance and verification

- **Real browser path:** Generate an equipped two-Being Encounter using the Empty ground profile. Demonstrate an actual player-issued defensive choice against a native opponent Thrust and a Defense-only opponent reacting appropriately to a player Thrust (use separate seeded runs if needed). Advance through preparation and impact using ordinary one-second controls. Display native outcomes; neither test requires a successful defense roll or HP change. Clearly identify any case where a legal prepared defense was actually allocated/executed, rather than just shown as a UI label.
- **Focused native tests:** Distinguish not-yet-ready, ready-held, used/consumed, no perceived threat, missing/unreadied necessary equipment, conflicting/pending Action, and simultaneous-boundary behavior as supported by the selected native contract. Verify no unearned skill XP or defensiveness, no duplicate Actions, and rollback/refusal without state/RNG change. Preserve the attack-only strategy, multi-actor movement and hex reach tests.
- **Persistence tests:** Save while defense is preparing/ready and while an incoming strike is pending; restore in a fresh session (preferably one new OS process), compare against uninterrupted native outcomes, Action/Choice IDs, equipment, native HP, observer evidence, and RNG. Patch NPC generation to fail on Load. Save and reload after resolution as well.
- **Validation discipline:** Run new/affected tests plus nearby native-defense, transaction, browser, and persistence regressions, with commands and elapsed times. Use `py_compile`, `git diff --check`, and a real Chrome smoke if environment permits. Do not run the ~1-hour full suite by default; do not claim unrun tests passed.
