## Outcome to deliver

Make an ordinary generated-Arena session support a **real, tactically consequential combat exchange in the shipped browser**, beyond the U-CAP-007 Draw → `not_facing` demonstration. A player should be able to understand and control facing, reach, movement, weapon preparation and the next lawful combat Action; the opponent should participate through an existing supported strategy when lawful. The Encounter must resolve at least one legal attack that **actually hits and applies native HP/protection/condition consequences** in a reproducible served-Chrome demonstration; outcomes are never faked or forced by the browser. Provide a usable continuation and inspectable combat feedback, including exact fresh-process Save/Load.

The **primary end-to-end acceptance** is one coherent, seeded encounter, played through the served browser and its actual controls, showing (1) generation with provenance and legitimately available combat prerequisites, (2) movement/facing and one or more real preparation Actions, (3) a successful resolved strike or other supported attack with a native, nonzero committed consequence, (4) at least one independent lawful opponent response/action if its supported chosen strategy makes one eligible, (5) player-authorized observations and Action/result history, (6) saving the resulting Encounter, restoring it in a *fresh Python OS process* without regeneration, and continuing legal play. Prefer also actual browser Load, but do not change extension permissions; if the browser file picker remains blocked, test the shipped HTTP/HTML Load contract and separately restore the exact browser-downloaded save, recording the limitation.

**Do not stop at isolated fixture green tests, only Action declaration, an out-of-reach/not-facing attack, or a native-only hit.** Use a deterministic seed or a transparently opt-in authored scenario to make the real exchange reproducible. If a seed-specific natural result is used, report the seed and true RNG/effect receipts. Never inject a hit, damage or combat state from JavaScript or after Encounter publication.
