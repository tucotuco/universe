## Controlling observational and discovery principles

1. **Reality / Observation / Belief are different.** Reality can hold actual Entities, locations, velocity, Actions and Events; an individual observer obtains evidence only through a legitimate perceptual/Action result. Belief must not be silently overwritten with objective data merely because the engine can query Reality. An observer's past observation is evidence about an earlier time, **not necessarily current fact**.
2. **Retain the initial Arena rule.** The currently unobstructed local Arena automatically perceives all Beings and Objects where no obstruction exists. Do **not** regress this into same-hex-only detection or force every obvious visible Being/Object through a six-second Spot roll. The currently adopted initial Arena simplified perception may deliver exact objective details for *perceptible* properties; this does not authorize projecting imperceptible private fields or exact facts after perception ceases. More general perception outside the Arena remains separate.
3. **Search, Spot and Listen are Actions, not skills** (adopted discovery rules): default discoverable Entity DC 15 unless explicitly authored, applicable d20 discovery check and racial Action aptitude, relevant Spotting/Hearing DC modifiers, no separate XP or levels. Deliberate attempts normally take **six seconds**, not reducible by combat-strategy timing. Search requires reachable/examinable access during that attempt; Spot requires physically possible line of sight; Listen requires physically audible evidence. Failed/impossible discovery does not reveal hidden facts. Preserve native transactional scheduling and common-snapshot resolution.
4. **Incidental** Spot/Listen may occur only on an independently meaningful environmental discovery opportunity. It costs no declared Action time, but it is **not** a player-spammable free check or an automatic retry every second after failure. Use the normal DC/racial/environmental rules where applicable. No pre-action knowledge of a simultaneously resolving Event can leak backward to influence that Event's original Action declaration.
5. **Entities versus Events:** a Being is an Object; an occurrence or Action resolution is event evidence, not an automatically created permanent Object. Surface location, movement/velocity and a Being's currently apparent Action **only if supported by perceptible source facts at the observation time**. Acceleration, visual uncertainty/noise, sound propagation, hidden intentions, omniscient future Actions, and inferred body/obstacle geometry are not adopted by implication. Preserve origin/receipt identity and event time without exposing internal IDs to a player.
6. **Strict persistence and atomicity:** observations, incidental check decisions, current versus stale evidence, multiple observers' different knowledge, RNG cursor and resulting Action legality must survive save/replay without accidental publication on refusal. Observer projections use opaque public handles and remain authorization-aware.
