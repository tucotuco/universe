# U-ARENA-016 — Adopted offensive Combat Actions

## Where we stand

This assignment is **partially delivered and remains incomplete**. The native Arena now accepts, resolves, records, displays, and saves two bounded offensive Actions: the one-second opposed-Being branch of **Touch**, and an armed **Feint** using a natively held/readied Dagger and advanced Dagger skill. Feint uses the normal Choice scheduler, frozen melee evaluation, Target-owned receipt, atomic Encounter publication, browser Action selector, and portable Arena file. A native fixture demonstrates a successful close-range Feint, its absence of damage, and the +2 on its next same-target strike. A generated browser test verifies the Feint control, accepted Choice, pending/completed cross-process Save/Load, and an actual out-of-reach result. This is an integrated expansion, but does not meet the assignment's broad-coverage success threshold.

The browser Touch path is: generate two Beings with an empty ground profile; activate the Encounter through Run; select the perceived opponent; choose Touch; advance one second; inspect the native committed outcome. At contact range the native evaluator rolls both d20s and compares the Actor's attack total to the target's defense total. A tie or lower Actor total is a miss. Touch has no damage effect. A position outside personal reach resolves as `out_of_reach` before drawing randomness. The browser Feint path additionally requires a ready held Dagger, advanced Dagger skill, and an activated Encounter; the player selects Swing or Thrust and sees the half-timing boundary, then advances to native resolution. A successful Feint grants +2 only to the immediately next accepted same-target MeleeStrike; choosing a different Action forfeits eligibility. Feint outcomes and timing are reconstructed from native Action/Choice state, with no exposed native IDs. The Arena-generated pending test happened to begin out of reach and therefore resolves `out_of_reach`; the native close-range fixture proves the success branch. Touch still implements only the unwilling opposing-Being branch; willingness, Dexterity-check branches, and Touch combined with another Action are not implemented. Feint currently implements only armed advanced-weapon use; unarmed Feint and movement/non-Choice forfeiture semantics remain unavailable.

## Shared implementation and adopted-rule handling

- Exact base `Action` event type `touch` is admitted only through a transactional Encounter. Scheduling requires two distinct participating, aware Beings, no instrument, and exactly one second. It uses the existing Choice/preparation protocol and consumes no random values on admission refusal.
- The evaluator uses the frozen Being dimensions and continuous native positions. It derives personal reach from the existing reference-arm function, then rolls the specified opposed d20 totals only when both Beings are placed and in reach. It records distance and one of the bounded Touch outcomes; it creates no damage/equipment effects. No optical path, body geometry, collision, or movement rule was added for Touch.
- Settlement rejects Touch attack/damage/defense effects and checks its limited outcome/metadata contract. The result is a native Action receipt in the ordinary committed Target history, not an Arena-side result.
- The Arena action catalog and shipped selector present the opposing-Being branch. The `/api/generated/touch` route accepts only a player-authorized handle; it does not expose native target or Action IDs. The view reports accepted/executing/completed state and outcome, and terminal tracing records the accepted Touch and committed result.
- The strict Arena snapshot tag closure now includes the already-adapted native `action.Action` type, so pending and completed Touch Actions reconstruct by native identity. No general checkpoint allowlist was broadened, no Action was regenerated/replayed, and no npcgen state was modified.
- Base native Action `feint` is admitted only through the reviewed transactional pathway with two participating, currently aware Beings, an actually held/readied supported weapon, advanced weapon skill, an explicit Swing/Thrust mode, exact half timing rounded up (then the Action's existing timing allocation), and the authoritative calculated melee reach. The frozen evaluator reuses the same melee reach/path, fatigue/DC, target facing/defense, and declared defense dependencies as MeleeStrike; it performs only the ordinary Attack Roll check, produces `success`/`failure`, and emits no damage, protection, or practice effect. Close-range success is verified at native boundary. A second use of the same source Feint is refused after the accepted follow-up strike, proving one-use consumption.
- A successful Feint's result stays in the native completed Action and Target history. The next scheduled `MeleeStrike` may reference that successful Feint only for the same Actor and Target; `MeleeStrike` consumes +2 to hit in the shared evaluator. Scheduling a different Action first makes that source unavailable. The follow-up strike stays at normal timing/reach. Snapshot/settlement validation distinguishes Feint's `success`/`failure` from ordinary MeleeStrike outcomes and rejects any Feint damage effect.
- The generated Arena Action selector exposes Feint only when the current native prerequisites pass, offers the weapon's supported Swing/Thrust modes with their derived durations, and routes through `/api/generated/feint`. Pending and completed Feint views are derived from native Choice/Action receipts, not an Arena-side simulation result. Pending and completed Feint checkpoints were restored in fresh Python processes and compared with uninterrupted state.
- No Save/Load guarantee is claimed for a Touch plus a still-pending movement plan: existing Arena movement-plan checkpoint admission only covers its established initial activation boundary. The new cross-process demonstration saves the pending Touch after activation with no additional movement plan.
