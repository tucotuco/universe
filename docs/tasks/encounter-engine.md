# Encounter engine, adopted rules, persistence and verification

Back to [TASKS.md](../../TASKS.md).

## Bounded demonstrated capabilities

- **One-second native transactional progression:** supported intervals advance movement before taking a common post-movement snapshot, evaluate due Actions, then atomically publish supported effects with rollback. This is not a guarantee for every combination. See [`src/encounter.py`](../../src/encounter.py), [`src/transactional_progression.py`](../../src/transactional_progression.py), and [`tests/test_transactional_progression.py`](../../tests/test_transactional_progression.py).
- **Target-owned consequences:** Action/effect records preserve causality; resulting HP, practice, history and state remain on Targets.
- **Deliberate Spot:** revision-60 six-second Action, one target-specific check (default discovery DC 15), no Spot skill and no Spot XP. It does not define category selection or general Attention. See [`src/activities.py`](../../src/activities.py) and [`tests/test_spot_group_evidence.py`](../../tests/test_spot_group_evidence.py).
- **Bounded identity-free Spot (U-SPOT-002A):** the existing `accept_spot_attention` adapter binds exactly one unfamiliar participating Being to the ordinary native Spot; zero or multiple candidates refuse before scheduling. The U-WORLD-001B integration demonstrates boundary-7 Awareness, failed-check behavior, and publisher rollback/retry. This does not define category selection, multi-match Search, Agent Attention, or unresolved-member selection. See [`src/attention_intent.py`](../../src/attention_intent.py), [`examples/individual_across_genealogy_population_place.py`](../../examples/individual_across_genealogy_population_place.py), and [`tests/test_genealogy_population_place.py`](../../tests/test_genealogy_population_place.py).
- **U-LOC-002A Being location evidence:** when the identity-free Spot is explicitly given its observer's `LocationEvidenceLedger`, successful native Spot stages one immutable `BeingLocationObservation` from the common post-movement/pre-action snapshot. The record is committed as a typed effect in the same publisher transaction as Awareness and lifecycle. Its owner-only last-seen query returns stored time, boundary, position and Action provenance without reading current Reality; later movement does not update it. Failure creates no record and observer-phase rollback restores both Awareness and evidence before seeded retry. Opted-in location recipients are explicitly outside portable checkpoint support. This does not add location sensing to ordinary unconfigured Spot, Groups, arbitrary Objects, maps or waypoints.
- **Group evidence:** 5E2C interim success can establish exact aggregate evidence about a supported fully visible Group. The separate `ObserveGroupAction` sampler retains provisional Spot-rank/XP behavior and needs a decision; it is not ordinary Spot.
- **Combat integration:** the bounded 5E2U example commits an Awareness-derived Thrust for 4 damage (HP 10→6) and 10 Dagger XP. Under 5E2M, Group envelopes describe aggregate extent and do not block melee; real modeled Beings/Objects still participate in physical obstruction.
- **Fresh-process continuation:** 5E1J6 restores one historical Run boundary and publishes one new Run boundary, with no imported-practice replay and native issuance for the new receipt. This Run-only scenario consumes no RNG. It does not demonstrate stochastic replay or general save/load.
- **U-ARENA-001 native Encounter Arena:** [`examples/npc_encounter_arena.py`](../../examples/npc_encounter_arena.py) imports two exact NPC Generator v1 source records through [`arena_npc_import.py`](../../src/arena_npc_import.py), binds them to distinct explicit native Being IDs, and resolves a six-second native Spot through the transactional Encounter publisher. The source names are display labels; the Human definitions, geometry, positions, and fixed Spot roll are Arena-authored. Only Ari receives Awareness on success; a failed roll publishes none. The demonstration is developer inspection, not an observer UI or portable save. [`tests/test_npc_encounter_arena.py`](../../tests/test_npc_encounter_arena.py) covers source validation/provenance, identity and collisions, property non-mapping, deterministic results, failure, and publisher rollback/retry.
- **U-NPC-BEING-001–004 (completed within scope):** [`src/npcgen_being_adapter.py`](../../src/npcgen_being_adapter.py) accepts only the exact external `npcgen-npc` v2 fixture (SHA-256 `e59b55a6c22f822d65716215b64bccf0c1f2c7a42d49036092580d7c470241ae`). Race/age/class/XP/possessions/family and other fields remain source metadata; native-only test mechanics remain explicit, and the record implies no Dagger ownership. The seeded run published a normal boundary-4 result with 4 body damage (Target HP 10→6). [`tests/test_npc_being_arena.py`](../../tests/test_npc_being_arena.py), [`tests/test_npc_being_arena_web.py`](../../tests/test_npc_being_arena_web.py), and [`tests/test_npcgen_being_integration.py`](../../tests/test_npcgen_being_integration.py) cover the native path, browser requests/refusals, and Dagger-rank-vs-zero control. This remains fixture-specific, does not calculate the full r60 strategy pool, and does not modify fixed Ari/Bex S6/S7 persistence or provide a general importer. See the [U-NPC-BEING-001 report](../reports/U-NPC-BEING-001-report.md), [U-NPC-BEING-002 report](../reports/U-NPC-BEING-002-report.md), [U-NPC-BEING-003 report](../reports/U-NPC-BEING-003-report.md), and [U-NPC-BEING-004 report](../reports/U-NPC-BEING-004-report.md).

- **U-NPC-BEING-005A (completed within scope):** Native Being `length`/stature is inches and `weight` is pounds; the 27-row `beings.tsv` source-foot values are migrated to an explicit `length_inches` schema, and `WeaponDictionary` converts the source weapon length column from feet to native inches. The exact v2 NPC fixture’s finite positive `state.height`/`state.weight`, cross-checked against two-decimal `sheet.age.height_inches`/`weight_pounds`, populate native stature and mass; physical measurements do not create geometry. The NPC developer views label inches/pounds and retain the independent authored Sphere and Dagger. `AgentDuel` computes measured personal reference arm length as `(15/37) × max(height,length)`, adds native weapon inches, converts to meters for impact comparison, and presents ranges in yards. A pure helper supports explicit size-category estimates including Medium 28 inches; the current AgentDuel does not infer a category from the existing `size_category()` stub. Its legacy shared-Reach replay mode remains unchanged. This does not migrate generic Object dimensions, ambiguous old literal fixture values, projectile/throwing calculations or rulebook prose; those need separate scoped work. See the [U-NPC-BEING-005A report](../reports/U-NPC-BEING-005A-report.md).
- **U-NPC-BEING-005B (completed within scope):** [`src/melee_combat.py`](../../src/melee_combat.py) now owns the shared measured Reach calculation: positive Being height/length select `(15/37) × max(height,length)` inches, the weapon's native inch length is added, and the result converts to meters for Encounter center-distance checks. [`AgentDuel`](../../src/ai_duel.py) reuses this helper while preserving its legacy replay path. [`MeleeStrike`](../../src/melee_combat.py) continues to evaluate reach against distance at impact in both the live and frozen transactional evaluators; this increment changes no publisher or resolution ordering. The NPC Arena now explicitly authors its positive thrust scene at 0.75 m with independent 0.25 m spheres, producing a normal boundary-4 native result with 4 body damage. At 1.5 m the committed result is `out_of_reach`, with no damage and no RNG advance. Tests cover the exact reach boundary, no geometry inference, meter-distance metadata, movement into/out of range before impact, native receipt boundaries, and the browser view. This does not alter the general `TacticalDuel` constant, projectile/throwing behavior, or adopted rules documents. See the [U-NPC-BEING-005B report](../reports/U-NPC-BEING-005B-report.md).
