## Completed bounded demonstrations

- **U-ARENA-001 candidate boundary:** two exact NPC Generator v1 exports are preserved as source bytes with engine, rule-hash, seed, edit, and content-digest provenance. An explicit per-Arena binding assigns each candidate a distinct native Being identity; only the source name becomes a display name. Native Human definitions and scene choices supply active attributes and placements. NPC race/sex/age/scores/class/level/XP/HP/dimensions/possessions/family remain source-only; the generator does not expose a discrete Universe skill list. This is not population membership, genealogy, observer knowledge, or cross-process binding persistence. See the [Arena example](../../examples/npc_encounter_arena.py) and [import boundary](../../src/arena_npc_import.py).

- `StatisticalGroup(ObjectInstance)` is a population-specific prototype, not a general Group ontology. Trusted world code can select one already-counted adult and atomically publish its realization while conserving Group totals.
- A supported successful Spot can record exact aggregate Group count evidence for one observer; it does not expose unresolved slot identifiers, individual identity or position.
- The 5E2S adapter accepts only one eligible unfamiliar participating Being; zero or several candidates are refused. It is an experiment, not general Agent Attention or category-directed Spot.
- U-SPOT-002A uses that adapter in the U-WORLD-001B integration. The invocation supplies only the acting Being; the adapter privately binds one unfamiliar participant to native Spot. The example explicitly authors initial awareness of the already-present peer so the later-realized Being is the only unfamiliar candidate. The target remains unknown until successful boundary-7 publication; failure and rollback/retry preserve the same native Spot rules.
- The 5E2U example connects Group count 28, independent trusted-world selection, next-boundary eligibility, later discovery and Awareness, then a native Thrust. No Group observation automatically triggers realization.
- Optional geometry supports a later ordinary Spot without changing Spot duration/DC/no-XP rules. Realization does not grant Awareness.

## COMPLETED bounded milestone: U-REL-001

The D1 audit and D2/D3 design contracts are complete. D2 records the confirmed exact unordered reproductive-parent-set semantics and gender-independent identities/roles. D3 adds the confirmed existence boundary: an instantiated reproductive group is nonempty, world-created only when resolution is demanded, and distinct from a potential/zero/unknown parent-set scope. D3's description of `ReproductionGroup` as a preferred name rather than an adopted type/storage model is historical: D4A/B and V1–V12 implemented world-owned ReproductionGroup records and their registry. The implementation uses `ReproductionGroupRecord` and `ReproductionGroupRegistry`; it does not establish a separate Entity subclass. D3 distinguishes complete parentage from offspring enumeration and explains why parent-global completeness must cover uninstantiated parent sets. D4A/B subsequently implemented the bounded authored-candidate and no-Encounter publication gates summarized below. Review [`family-generation-u-rel-001-d3-completeness.md`](../design/family-generation-u-rel-001-d3-completeness.md), alongside the [D2 contract](../design/family-generation-u-rel-001-d2-contract.md) and [D1 source audit](../design/family-generation-u-rel-001-audit.md) for the adopted design constraints.

**V10R foundational correction:** ReproductionGroup membership itself is the sole structural authority for reproductive parentage. A nonempty group can be published with one or more known parent members and `parents_complete=false`; this is positive known parentage, not a claim that those members are exhaustive. Membership can be added later and explicitly marked complete without changing the ReproductionGroup or offspring identities. A complete singleton is valid. The offspring-to-known-parent lookup is only a derived index from group membership, not a separate parentage ledger. Distinct incomplete groups with overlapping known parents remain distinct; the completed exact-parent-set index includes only complete groups. Parent-set completeness remains independent of offspring enumeration completeness.

The Being model still has no age/date-of-birth field or built-in relationship graph. ReproductionGroup records store offspring as logical Person IDs. V5/V6 add a bounded, explicit bridge that can materialize a supported logical offspring as a same-ID native BeingInstance; U-WORLD-001A/B then demonstrate one authored adult-slot/Encounter binding and observer-specific Spot. These paths do not materialize every offspring or infer Being properties, population membership, location, or observer knowledge from parentage. `StatisticalGroup` has stable latent slot IDs and aggregate counts; its Encounter member-realization transaction remains a separate subsystem, connected only by the explicit bounded bridge described below. D1's edge-only B is a historical design fixture; one edge does not instantiate a nonempty reproductive group or prove its full parent set. D3's pure authored-candidate and world-publication gates are implemented in D4A/B. Global offspring closure is unresolved without proof that the relevant parent-set domain, including uninstantiated sets, is closed. World generation changes no observer knowledge.

D4A's candidate-only implementation adds `ReproductionGroupProposal` and its prepared candidate as immutable logical-ID records; the pure preparer canonicalizes exact parent sets and refuses empty/duplicate identities. The candidate cannot claim complete offspring enumeration. It performs no registration, reservation, Reality publication, RNG use, date handling, observation, Being construction or population coupling. This is a partial engineering milestone, not a world-level family generator.

D4B adds a `Universe`-owned, in-memory registry and typed publisher for authored nonempty candidates and their offspring parentage facts. It checks per-world exact-parent-set and group-ID uniqueness, Person-ID assignments, request idempotency and registry revision; failure injection verifies rollback. It is explicitly no-Encounter and nonpersistent.

V1 adds a runnable no-Encounter seeded resolution example using an explicitly supplied `TestOnlyOffspringCountDistribution`. It samples a nonnegative count, including zero, and distinct ordered abstract origin ticks within an inclusive caller-bounded horizon. Positive outcomes publish a unique group, stable logical offspring IDs, parentage and tick facts together. Zero outcomes publish only a record scoped to that parent-set request and horizon; no group or Person IDs are created. Distribution schema and sampling algorithm versions are recorded. Preparation uses a private seeded RNG, and publication is revision-checked, idempotent and fault-injection rollback-tested.

V2 adds [`progressive_statistical_reproduction.py`](../../examples/progressive_statistical_reproduction.py): an explicitly test-only, versioned per-interval count profile over abstract integer period indices. It resolves periods 0 through T1, then atomically appends only T1+1 through T2; hash-derived period streams and IDs make that path match a fresh direct T2 resolution. Existing prefix facts are checked and retained. A zero-only prefix creates no empty ReproductionGroup; a later positive period creates the one group for that exact parent set. Overlapping horizons, profile/seed changes and conflicts with authored/V1 scopes refuse explicitly. Tests cover initial and extension rollback, idempotency, stable identities, singleton parents and the absence of Encounter, Being, population, observer, Belief or global-RNG side effects. Neither V1 weights nor V2 interval weights or integer ticks are adopted biological/calendar rules. Neither implementation infers global offspring completeness, creates Beings, alters population, or changes Awareness/Observation/Belief. Durable cross-process identity and biological meaning remain unsupported.

V3 adds [`multigenerational_reproduction_on_demand.py`](../../examples/multigenerational_reproduction_on_demand.py), reusing V2's publisher for three overlapping exact parent sets. Generated logical offspring can become parents in a later group. The detached registry snapshot derives known exact parentage, known full siblings, two-parent/one-shared-parent half siblings, relationship-neutral shared-parent facts, and known two-step grandparent paths. Publication refuses an edge that closes a known ancestry cycle. The example includes positive-to-positive extension, a zero-only request, and one-parent generation. Queries do not mutate the world and make no claim about ungenerated groups or undiscovered relatives. All profiles and integer periods remain test-only; no Being, population, Encounter, observer, Belief, calendar, or adopted biological rule is introduced.

V4 adds [`reproduction_checkpoint_continuation.py`](../../examples/reproduction_checkpoint_continuation.py) and a versioned JSON checkpoint scoped strictly to the authoritative reproduction registry. It carries authored, V1, and progressive V2 records, exact parent-set and identity indexes, period outcomes, idempotency records, revisions, profile/sampler identifiers, and partial-enumeration metadata. Restore validates detached data before atomically populating a compatible fresh Universe while preserving registry container identities and rebinding process-local owner IDs. A separate Python process restores the V3 genealogy, retries a committed request without another draw, extends an existing parent set, and extends a saved zero-only scope to positive. Tests cover malformed/incompatible inputs, target refusal, every restore fault phase, later-publication rollback and deterministic retry. The demonstration compares the extended restoration with an uninterrupted same-seed request sequence, including the logical records and derived kinship queries. The format's SHA-256 digest detects accidental corruption; it is not authentication. This is not `Universe` save/load, Encounter persistence, native Being reconstruction, biological/calendar semantics, or a complete-family claim.

V5 adds [`reproduction_being_identity_bridge.py`](../../examples/reproduction_being_identity_bridge.py) and a narrow world-owned operation that materializes one already-established logical offspring ID as a real `BeingInstance` with exactly that ID. It uses the existing Being constructor and `Universe.add_object()` registration path, taking an explicitly authored `BeingDefinition`; no physical or reproductive properties are inferred from generation data. Request retries return the same native instance. Collision, stale-revision, wrong-reference, and alternate-binding requests refuse. Fault injection across ObjectRegistry, binding-index, request-index, and consistency publication points proves in-place rollback while preserving earlier Objects/Beings, genealogy, container identities, and RNG. An optional V4 reproduction-only restore is followed by a separately authored Being construction; native Being state is not in the checkpoint. The bridge does not add population/StatisticalGroup membership, position, Encounter participation, Observation, Awareness, or Belief. Native Beings sharing a logical Person ID are not included by the V4 checkpoint; it refuses that mixed state.

V6 adds [`reproduction_being_checkpoint_continuation.py`](../../examples/reproduction_being_checkpoint_continuation.py) and a separately versioned combined checkpoint for the complete supported reproduction registry plus exactly one native Being binding. It reconstructs a genuine native `BeingInstance` from the checkpoint's closed `BeingDefinition` data, with the same logical offspring ID and ObjectRegistry identity, then continues a progressive generation in a fresh Python process and matches an uninterrupted same-seed control. The original V4 reproduction-only export still refuses after native materialization. Combined restore is limited to a pristine authored Being: unchanged original/current definition, default strategy/exertion/fatigue, no practice or personal history, no inventory, position, geometry or occlusion, and no other native Objects or non-origin Events. Unknown, corrupted, colliding, incompatible or unsupported records refuse before publication; injected failures across reproductive restore, native registration, binding/request indexes and final consistency roll back in place. The digest detects accidental corruption, not authenticity. This remains world-only and grants no Agent knowledge; it does not add population/Encounter state, general Universe persistence, biology, or calendar semantics.
