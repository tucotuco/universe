## Completed bounded demonstrations

- `StatisticalGroup(ObjectInstance)` is a population-specific prototype, not a general Group ontology. Trusted world code can select one already-counted adult and atomically publish its realization while conserving Group totals.
- A supported successful Spot can record exact aggregate Group count evidence for one observer; it does not expose unresolved slot identifiers, individual identity or position.
- The 5E2S adapter accepts only one eligible unfamiliar participating Being; zero or several candidates are refused. It is an experiment, not general Agent Attention or category-directed Spot.
- The 5E2U example connects Group count 28, independent trusted-world selection, next-boundary eligibility, later discovery and Awareness, then a native Thrust. No Group observation automatically triggers realization.
- Optional geometry supports a later ordinary Spot without changing Spot duration/DC/no-XP rules. Realization does not grant Awareness.

## ACTIVE: U-REL-001

The D1 audit and D2/D3 design contracts are complete; none authorizes production implementation. D2 records the confirmed exact unordered reproductive-parent-set semantics and gender-independent identities/roles. D3 adds the confirmed existence boundary: an instantiated reproductive group is nonempty, world-created only when resolution is demanded, and distinct from a potential/zero/unknown parent-set scope. `ReproductionGroup` is the preferred working name, not an adopted type or storage model. D3 distinguishes complete parentage from offspring enumeration and explains why parent-global completeness must cover uninstantiated parent sets. Review [`family-generation-u-rel-001-d3-completeness.md`](../design/family-generation-u-rel-001-d3-completeness.md), alongside the [D2 contract](../design/family-generation-u-rel-001-d2-contract.md) and [D1 source audit](../design/family-generation-u-rel-001-audit.md), before approving a first no-Encounter authored RG0 candidate/commit gate. No generation, group, or observation behavior is implemented.

The current Being model has no age/date-of-birth or relationship graph. `StatisticalGroup` has stable latent slot IDs and aggregate counts, but child Being realization is unsupported. The audited transaction has one typed adult-realization operation, not a general relationship-write operation; it is Encounter-owned, not a no-Encounter world publisher. D1's edge-only B is a later integration fixture only: one edge does not instantiate a nonempty reproductive group or prove its full parent set. D3 recommends a pure authored RG0 candidate plus narrow world-owned atomic commit and a separate zero-through-T test that creates no ReproductionGroup. Global offspring closure is unresolved without proof that the relevant parent-set domain, including uninstantiated sets, is closed. World generation changes no observer knowledge.
