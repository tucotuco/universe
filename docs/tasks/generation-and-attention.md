## Completed bounded demonstrations

- `StatisticalGroup(ObjectInstance)` is a population-specific prototype, not a general Group ontology. Trusted world code can select one already-counted adult and atomically publish its realization while conserving Group totals.
- A supported successful Spot can record exact aggregate Group count evidence for one observer; it does not expose unresolved slot identifiers, individual identity or position.
- The 5E2S adapter accepts only one eligible unfamiliar participating Being; zero or several candidates are refused. It is an experiment, not general Agent Attention or category-directed Spot.
- The 5E2U example connects Group count 28, independent trusted-world selection, next-boundary eligibility, later discovery and Awareness, then a native Thrust. No Group observation automatically triggers realization.
- Optional geometry supports a later ordinary Spot without changing Spot duration/DC/no-XP rules. Realization does not grant Awareness.

## ACTIVE: U-REL-001

The D1 design/source audit and D2 FamilyGroup contract are complete. D2 records the user-confirmed exact unordered reproductive-parent-set rule, gender-independent identities/roles, cross-FamilyGroup derived sibling relationships, and world-only generation with no automatic Agent knowledge. Storage and mechanics remain proposals; no production implementation is authorized. The leading design candidate is a first-class world-level `FamilyGroup`, distinct from population Groups, household/social links and Being instantiation. D2 compares the competing ontology and resolution scopes in [`family-generation-u-rel-001-d2-contract.md`](../design/family-generation-u-rel-001-d2-contract.md); D1's implementation evidence remains in [`family-generation-u-rel-001-audit.md`](../design/family-generation-u-rel-001-audit.md). Before implementation, resolve the remaining questions in D2: FamilyGroup and latent identity storage, event/time rules, offspring-origin multiplicity, scoped/global completeness, population accounting, RNG guarantees and no-Encounter transaction ownership.

The current Being model has no age/date-of-birth or relationship graph. `StatisticalGroup` has stable latent slot IDs and aggregate counts, but child Being realization is unsupported. The audited transaction has one typed adult-realization operation, not a general relationship-write operation. D1's edge-only B is an initial validator fixture only: one edge does not establish a complete exact parent set or FamilyGroup, and the assignment explicitly says it must not become the permanent public API. D2's later gates proceed from pure validation to FamilyGroup identity/completeness and a separate no-Encounter atomic publisher. World generation changes no observer knowledge.

- **U-ATT-001:** ongoing mutable Attention may eventually concern categories, known Beings/Objects/Groups, or Events such as moonrise; it does not guarantee discovery and differs from a focused Spot Action.
