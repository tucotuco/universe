# Native history bridge — current findings

**Status: U-PER-SCALE-001 remains ACTIVE and incomplete.** Native Save/Load now preserves logical ReproductionGroup records and registered Being bindings by stable ID, selected Group, PersonalHistory, observation, combat, movement, pending Actions, `/10`–`/12` migration slices and generated Arena activation. A new native initial-Grapple→Takedown continuation exposed and fixed random automatic-Hold Action identity across fresh-process restore. These are still selected capabilities: the architecture and family matrix remain far from the full acceptance gate.

## Problems first

- The new native Save still stores both a whole committed receipt/Target object graph and a fact-history log. It is functional but not the requested compact snapshot + lossless independent history architecture. The tested 64-Action native Saves are 7.68/8.01/11.24 MB at 2/20/200 registered Beings; the current graph remains larger than the independent fact history. A 200-quiet-boundary history was reduced 86.28% by eliminating repeated RNG tuples, but the graph is still about 4.84 MB. No full proportional-growth or cutover claim is made.
- Family closure remains incomplete. The native tests cover pending Delay, Dagger MeleeStrike, Stun interruption/recovery, StatisticalGroup facts, two observer-owned Group observations, ReproductionGroup logical facts and ObjectRegistry-bound Being identity, one PersonalHistory fact/validator continuation, and literal `EpisodeFacts` plus a bound listener account for completed AskAboutPast. Reproduction v3 alias/merge history and linked-Being identity pass an exact post-Load request retry and native Delay continuation; new materialization remains governed by the existing no-Encounter context rule, and other realization workflows remain open. Other supported Actions, movement/equipment variations, PersonalHistory mutation/query parity, Arena-perception observer APIs, cross-Encounter ownership and historical consumer parity remain unverified or unsupported. LocationEvidenceLedger and ObjectObservationLedger now have focused fresh-process tests.
- `export_save()` can persist external observer recipients only when the host passes explicit `RecipientBinding` values. Missing bindings fail closed. Ordinary app/Arena Save/Load has not been cut over to this API; `/12` behavior remains unchanged.
- The portable graph is source-compatible and includes Library definitions through its graph, but the new native schema has not yet demonstrated legacy `/10`–`/12` migration, the complete adversarial/corruption portfolio, or crash recovery for an incremental durable journal.
- There is no adopted-rule ambiguity or external dependency blocking this work. The remaining scope is engineering: expand safe family adapters/tests, normalize history without losing inspection semantics, prove scale and compatibility, then integrate behind the existing reader.

## Problems first — StatisticalGroup membership history adapter

A source-linked Group membership scenario initially failed strict native Save in two distinct ways. First, `MembershipChange` records were omitted from the native exact-type/field registries, so a Group with a dated join/leave sequence failed closed as `unsupported_native_value`. After adding the record schema, exercising the intended `StatisticalGroup.state_at()` reader exposed that `MembershipChange` lacked its documented dictionary projection method. A first fixture that registered the Group as an Encounter Entity also failed with `unreceipted position change`: independently advancing a Group that is an Encounter inspection target mutates its represented spatial state outside an Encounter receipt. The fixture was corrected to retain the Group in the Universe registry without adding it to the Encounter. This preserves the modeled distinction: Group-owned time/motion and membership facts are independently persisted; a Being join does not imply travel or alter Encounter position.

The native closed registry now includes the exact five-field `MembershipChange` dataclass. `MembershipChange.to_dict()` restores the projection consumed by `state_at()`. A pure validation function is called during both strict Save capture and native graph rehydration; it checks finite nondecreasing in-range times, Group and registered Being identities, supported adult category, legal alternating join/leave transitions, and agreement between history and live incoming/departed membership. It does not create Encounter Events or infer Being movement. Invalid owner, future-time, missing-Being, unsupported-category and repeated-transition records are refused read-only.

`test_statistical_group_facts_and_keyed_realization_resume_in_fresh_process` saves after join at t=0, leave at t=1 and rejoin at t=3, compares `state_at()` at five cuts, then independently advances Group motion and a native Encounter Delay in parent and fresh OS process. Group time/position/velocity, an original materialized member moving with its Group, and an incoming Being that stays at its own position all match; RNG and Encounter history also match. `test_native_membership_history_validation_is_ordered_and_read_only` exercises malformed facts. Exact focused counts and the full native persistence result are recorded in the current engineering report. The exact `GroupMember` stable ID/offset adapter is now included and the same fresh-process case also exercises one original adult realization. This closes the tested membership and adult-member persistence path only. Exact verification: native persistence **40 / 117.186s**; focused fresh-process Group case **1 / 1.809s**; Group membership **12 / 0.128s**; Group evolution **8 / 0.106s**; historical Group realization **5 / 0.029s**; checkpoint adapters **50 / 19.038s**; capability matrix **3 / 0.009s**. ## Current architecture and verified behavior

`src/native_history.py` records an initial inspection projection, ordered atomic presence/value deltas, Action/Event/Effect causality, timing, RNG state, target/objective facts and observer projections. `NativeHistoryRecorder` appends after successful native publication only. First native export idempotently attaches a recorder; strict load verifies and restores the authenticated recorder so future committed boundaries append automatically. It does not resolve Actions. Native query integrity, preimages, order, duplicate IDs, append ownership and rollback behavior have focused tests.

`src/native_persistence.py` is a headless byte/file API. It uses `src/native_graph_snapshot.py` with schema `universe.native-encounter-graph/1`, not the Arena `/12` tag allowlist. It strictly checks the closed `FIELD_ADAPTERS` schema, graph references, portable callback recipe vocabulary, durable observer bindings, canonical bytes, duplicate keys, size/depth/node bounds and outer digest. Restore preserves logical IDs, reconstructs `StatisticalGroup` keyed RNG, `RealityLedger` validators, `PersonalHistory` validators, recipient identity anchors, ReproductionGroup facts and Being request bindings, native receipts and progression ownership without rerunning Actions. A separate combined Reproduction/Being checkpoint advances to v4 to represent the already implemented `BeingInstance.size_category` field while retaining strict v1–v3 readers. The old `src/arena_native_snapshot.py` import remains a compatibility shim for unchanged `/12` callers.

The supported `RunAction` family now has a real mid-run native-save case. A Run starting at t=0 captures its start position, moves to x=.5 at t=1, saves with the Action still unresolved, then restores in a fresh OS process and advances normally to x=1 at t=2. The result matches uninterrupted execution for Action outcome, PracticeEffect source Action ID, earned XP, skill-use facts, RNG, committed receipt IDs, and full native-history digest. `NativeHistoryQuery.entity_context_at()` also returns the position facts at both checked boundaries. This adds a directly exercised noncombat movement Action to native continuation coverage; it does not close generic movement paths, all cancellation/interruption variants, or graph-free history reconstruction.
